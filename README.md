# Privacy Policy for Trivia Party: Quiz Game

**Effective date: September 30, 2026**

Silicon Genie built **Trivia Party: Quiz Game** as a trivia game available through Google Play.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

## Information We Collect

Trivia Party: Quiz Game does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

Based on the current version of the app, gameplay settings and preferences are stored locally on your device. This includes information such as:

- Daily game usage count
- Music and sound-effect preferences
- Ultra purchase entitlement status

This locally stored information is used only to provide the app's features and preferences.

## In-App Purchases

Trivia Party: Quiz Game offers an optional **Ultra** one-time in-app purchase.

Purchases are processed through **Google Play Billing**. The app communicates with Google Play to obtain product information, initiate purchases, verify purchase status, and restore purchases.

Silicon Genie does not directly collect or process your payment card information. Payment and purchase information handled by Google is subject to Google's own privacy policies and terms.

## Advertising and Analytics

The current version of Trivia Party: Quiz Game does not include advertising or third-party analytics services.

## Device Permissions

The current version of the app does not request access to sensitive device information such as your precise location, contacts, camera, or microphone for gameplay.

## Children's Privacy

Trivia Party: Quiz Game does not knowingly collect personal information directly from users through the app.

If you believe personal information has been collected in error, please contact Silicon Genie so the matter can be reviewed.

## Data Sharing

Silicon Genie does not sell personal information.

Information necessary to process an in-app purchase may be handled by Google Play as part of the Google Play Billing service.

## Data Retention

Gameplay preferences and usage information described above are stored locally on the user's device.

This local information may be removed when the app is uninstalled or its application data is cleared, subject to Android device behavior.

Purchase records may be retained by Google according to Google's own policies.

## Security

Reasonable measures are used to limit the information handled by the app. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Third-Party Services

The app uses Google Play services for in-app purchase functionality.

Google Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
